## What This Is

The CALCULA Colony is a multi-agent governance framework built on top of the OpenClaw multi-agent runtime. It consists of 7 specialized agents, each contributing a full essay representing their domain of expertise within the colony's mission.

The colony operates under the **CALCULA engine** — a composite evaluation system that scores solutions not by profit, but by:

- 🌱 Flourishing (25%)
- 🛡️ Harm Reduction (20%)
- ⚖️ Equity (20%)
- ♻️ Regenerative Capacity (15%)
- 🤝 Cooperation (12%)
- ✨ Beauty (8%)

All decisions are gated by the **Aethel Safety Kernel** (Rust), enforcing sovereignty, love quality, and extraction-free standards at the code level.
